Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Revenue for the quarter increased 8% year-over-year, driven primarily by strength in our cloud
services segment, though growth moderated compared to the prior quarter's 14% increase. We are
revising our full-year revenue guidance downward to reflect softer enterprise demand in Europe
and continued foreign exchange headwinds. We now expect full-year revenue growth in the range of
5% to 7%, compared to our previous guidance of 8% to 10%.

Gross margin for the quarter was 62.4%, down from 64.1% in the prior year period, primarily due
to increased cloud infrastructure costs and a higher mix of lower-margin hardware revenue. We
expect margin pressure to persist through the next two quarters as we continue to invest in data
center capacity ahead of anticipated demand recovery.

Operating expenses increased 11% year-over-year, reflecting continued investment in research and
development, particularly in artificial intelligence infrastructure and go-to-market expansion
for our newer product lines. We remain committed to these investments despite near-term margin
impact, as we believe they position the company well for long-term growth.

Management commentary during the earnings call struck a cautious but not alarmed tone. The CFO
noted, "We're seeing real macro pressure in a couple of key verticals, but our pipeline for next
year gives us confidence in the underlying demand story." The CEO added that the company was
"leaning into the investment cycle now so we're not playing catch-up later," while acknowledging
that "the next couple of quarters will likely be choppier than we'd like."

Liquidity and Capital Resources

As of the end of the quarter, we had cash and cash equivalents of $4.2 billion, compared to $4.6
billion at the end of the prior fiscal year. The decrease was primarily attributable to increased
capital expenditures related to data center expansion and continued share repurchases under our
existing buyback program. We believe our current cash position, together with cash generated from
operations, will be sufficient to meet our working capital and capital expenditure requirements
for at least the next twelve months.

Risk Factors (excerpt)

Our results are subject to fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates, particularly the euro
and British pound, given our significant international revenue exposure. Continued macroeconomic
uncertainty in Europe could further pressure enterprise spending and adversely affect our ability
to meet revised guidance.
